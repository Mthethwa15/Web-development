Our Services

Kitchen Support

Our Kitchen Support services are about serving our local community and those who are less fortunate within it. We provide food to the homeless and struggling families.

Food Packs

In our Food Pack program we work on delivering to wider range of communites within the province of Gauteng such Soshanguve, Soweto, Ga-rankuwa & Atteridgeville to individuals and families in need.

School Projects

For our School Projects, we work on donating food to preschools, primary schools and highschools for those who are unable to have breakfast at homes and carry lunch to school, even to families that try spare a bit of change to improve their financial situation at home.
